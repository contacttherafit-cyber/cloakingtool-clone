FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CloakingTool.

What is cloaking?
Cloaking is the practice of showing different content to different visitors. Bots and reviewers see a "safe" page while real users see your actual offer.
How does bot detection work?
The engine analyzes User-Agent strings, IP addresses, device fingerprints, and behavioral signals in real-time.
Can I use it with my existing tracker?
Yes. CloakingTool integrates seamlessly with Keitaro, Voluum, ClickDealer, and any platform that supports standard tracking links.
Is it really free?
Yes! No credit card required. No limits on traffic volume. Fully open access for everyone.
